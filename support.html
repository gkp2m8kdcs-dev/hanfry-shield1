Getting started

Open hanfry-shield, tap the Markets or Discover tab, then use the search field at the top. Type a ticker symbol (for example, AAPL) or a company name. Tap the result to open the stock detail page, then use the heart icon to add it to your watchlist or the buy/sell sheet to record a transaction.
Go to the Portfolio tab, tap the "+" button in the top corner, name your portfolio, set an optional goal, and choose a base currency. Once created, you can record buy and sell transactions from the stock detail page or directly from the portfolio.
hanfry-shield includes coverage for NASDAQ, NYSE, LSE (London), TSE (Tokyo), HKEX (Hong Kong), ASX (Australia), and TSX (Toronto). Symbols from these exchanges can be searched, added to watchlists, and tracked in any portfolio.

Data, backup & export

All data created in hanfry-shield — watchlists, portfolios, transactions, alerts, photos, notes — is stored inside the application's encrypted sandbox on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to our servers, and there is no cloud sync.
The simplest backup is to enable iCloud Backup for your device in iOS Settings — Apple's encrypted iCloud Backup includes application sandboxes when enabled. For a portable, human-readable backup, use the in-app CSV export feature in Settings → Data Export, then save the resulting file to the Files app, email it to yourself, or copy it to another location you control.
Open Settings → Data Export. Choose the data set you want to export (transactions, alerts, watchlist), then tap Export. iOS will offer the standard share sheet so you can save the file to Files, send it via Mail, or pass it to any other destination. The CSV file is generated entirely on-device.

Price alerts

From a stock detail page, tap the alert (flag) icon, set a price target above or below the current price, and save. hanfry-shield evaluates the alert locally on your device using its on-device data, and delivers the notification through iOS when the target is crossed. We do not run a push notification server.
Check that iOS notifications are enabled for hanfry-shield (Settings → Notifications → hanfry-shield). Confirm the alert is still listed in Settings → Price Alerts and is not marked as triggered or paused. If Focus mode is enabled, alerts may be silenced — adjust Focus settings or allow notifications from hanfry-shield during your active Focus.

Photo vault

When recording a buy or sell transaction, scroll to the photo attachment row and tap the camera icon to take a new photo, or the gallery icon to import an existing one. You will be prompted for the relevant iOS permission the first time. The image is copied into the application sandbox and linked to that transaction.
No. Photos attached in hanfry-shield stay inside the application sandbox. We do not have access to them, and they are not synced to any cloud service by the application. If your device backs up to iCloud, the encrypted iCloud Backup may include them, governed by Apple's privacy practices.

Privacy

No. hanfry-shield has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting, no account system, and no cloud sync. The full privacy policy is available at privacy.html.

Account & sync

hanfry-shield is designed as a private, single-device workspace. An account would require us to store identifiers and transmit data to a server, which is incompatible with the application's privacy-first design. If you switch devices, you can restore your data from an iCloud Backup or by importing a previously exported CSV file.
