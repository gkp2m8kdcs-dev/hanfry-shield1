// CAPABILITIES

Investor-grade tools, end to end.

Every screen is built around the workflow of someone who actually holds positions — not a feed of social sentiment.

◈
Interactive K-line Engine
Six timeframes with crosshair inspection. Pinch, drag, and scrub through years of price action with monospaced precision.
◐
Multi-Portfolio Ledger
Track multiple portfolios with goal tracking, donut allocation breakdowns, and full transaction history including dividends, splits, and fees.
⬚
Photo Vault
Attach receipts and chart snapshots directly to transactions. Captured from camera or library, organized by symbol, encrypted on-device.
⚐
Local Price Alerts
Set targets on your watchlist. Alerts fire when crossed, evaluated entirely on-device — no servers, no push notifications from the cloud.
∑
DCF Valuation
Run a discounted cash flow model with editable assumptions. Includes a compound interest calculator and position size by account risk.
◉
Risk Profile
A six-question questionnaire that recommends an asset allocation matched to your temperament and time horizon.
▤
News with Deep Links
Market, earnings, economy, technology, healthcare, crypto, and commodities coverage — every related symbol opens directly into its stock page.
↧
CSV Export
Export every transaction, alert, and position to CSV at any time. Your data is yours — always.
⌘
Editorial Design
A dark editorial theme with gold accents, monospaced digits, and tactile micro-interactions. Built for focus, not distraction.
