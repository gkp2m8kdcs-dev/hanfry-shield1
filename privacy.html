Privacy Policy

App: hanfry-shield · Effective: January 1, 2026 · Version 1.0

Short version

hanfry-shield does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no cloud sync, and no analytics SDK. All portfolios, transactions, alerts, and photos you create are stored locally and encrypted on your iPhone. No data collection

1. Scope of this policy

This privacy policy applies to the hanfry-shield mobile application distributed on the Apple App Store under the name "hanfry-shield". It describes how the application handles information when you install, use, and interact with it on your device.

hanfry-shield is published as an individual developer project. References to "we", "us", and "our" refer to the publisher of hanfry-shield, reachable at the email address listed in the Contact section below.

2. What we collect

We collect nothing. hanfry-shield does not collect personal data, usage data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, advertising IDs (IDFA), location data, contact data, or any other information from your device.

The application is designed to operate entirely offline with respect to our servers. There are no analytics SDKs (such as Firebase, Amplitude, Mixpanel, or equivalent) embedded in the app, no crash reporting services that phone home, no advertising frameworks, no tracking pixels, no A/B testing infrastructure, and no remote configuration fetches.

2.1 Data you create inside the app

All data you create — including watchlists, portfolios, transactions, price alerts, attached photos, notes, and valuation inputs — is stored only on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

2.2 Diagnostic data

hanfry-shield does not submit crash reports or performance telemetry to us. Apple may collect anonymous, aggregated crash reports on its own initiative if you have opted in to share analytics with Apple in your iOS Settings; this is controlled by Apple, not by hanfry-shield.

3. How your data is stored

All user-created content in hanfry-shield is stored inside the application's sandboxed local documents directory. This directory is protected by iOS's standard file-system protections and is only accessible to the hanfry-shield application itself.

Photo attachments captured through the camera or imported from your photo library are stored alongside the relevant transaction record inside the application sandbox. They are not written to your shared photo library, not uploaded anywhere, and not exposed to other applications.

When you delete a portfolio, transaction, alert, or photo from hanfry-shield, the underlying record is removed from local storage. Uninstalling the application permanently deletes everything associated with hanfry-shield from your device.

3.1 Data export

hanfry-shield includes a built-in CSV export feature that lets you save your transaction history, alerts, and watchlist to a file of your choosing (for example, to the Files app, Mail, or another share target you control). Export is performed entirely on-device. We do not receive, view, or store the exported file.

3.2 iCloud and backups

hanfry-shield does not write its data to iCloud or to any other cloud service. If your device is configured to back up the application sandbox to iCloud (an iOS-level setting managed by Apple), a backup copy may exist inside your encrypted iCloud backup. That backup is governed by Apple's privacy and security practices, not ours, and is accessible only to you through your Apple ID.

4. Third-party services

hanfry-shield does not integrate third-party SDKs, APIs, analytics services, advertising networks, attribution platforms, or social login providers. There are no third-party cookies, no remote fonts loaded over the network at runtime within the app, and no embedded web views that contact external servers.

Market data, news content, and fundamental data presented inside hanfry-shield are bundled or generated for demonstration purposes and do not involve outbound network calls to our infrastructure.

5. iOS permissions we request

hanfry-shield requests only the iOS permissions that are strictly necessary for the features you use. Each permission is requested at the moment you invoke the corresponding feature, never at first launch.

5.1 Camera

Used to capture a photo of a receipt, trade confirmation, or chart snapshot to attach to a transaction. Photos remain inside the application sandbox. We do not receive them.

5.2 Photo Library (read-only)

Used to import an existing photo from your library to attach to a transaction. The selected photo is copied into the application sandbox; the original remains untouched in your library.

5.3 Notifications

Used to deliver local price alerts. Notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely by iOS on the device. We do not operate a push notification server.

5.4 Face ID / Touch ID

If you choose to enable biometric protection for the application, iOS will prompt you for Face ID or Touch ID before unlocking the app. Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS in the Secure Enclave; hanfry-shield never accesses the raw biometric template.

You may revoke any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Children's privacy

hanfry-shield is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because the application collects no personal data from anyone, no special protections for minors are required, but we do not knowingly market the app to children.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Effective" date at the top of this document and, where appropriate, surface a notice inside the application. Because hanfry-shield does not contact our servers, we cannot push notifications about policy changes; please review this page periodically.

Because we do not collect contact information, we cannot email you directly about policy updates.
